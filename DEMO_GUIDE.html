OWL — 15-Minute Demo Guide 🦉

Say once, up front: "Not eco-warriors — we just dislike waste. If it can't be measured, it shouldn't be asserted."  Audience: CTOs / operators / infra. OWL = a bench measuring real device-layer energy of streaming & AI.

1 · Open (1m)

  • GoS = neutral, technically credible French NGO
  • OWL measures real watts, not models / estimates
  • Scope honesty: device layer only — network / CDN / datacenter excluded

2 · Measurement spine (2m)

  • Smart plug (full mW) + CPU/GPU thermal sensors
  • Protocol: baseline → locked task → poll → ΔW, ΔE
  • Traffic Light Confidence — every claim 🟢/🟡/🔴
  • "the number comes with its own uncertainty"

3 · Video codec compare (3m) — the meat

  • Same clip, multiple codecs / encoders head-to-head
  • Energy and quality (VMAF) side by side
  • ⭐ AV1 hardware vs software at same bitrate — energy↔quality tradeoff, measured
  • "hw encode saves energy — but does it cost quality? Now we can answer."

4 · AI tethered to streaming (3m)

  • AI = the new load on the same infrastructure
  • /llm/compare — tiny → 20B, energy per token, CPU vs GPU
  • /image/compare — N models side by side, first-run cost
  • "same rigor, applied to the AI now sharing the pipe"

5 · Findings catalog (2m)

  • /findings — a growing evidence base, not one-off demos
  • Each finding cites a real stored measurement on disk
  • "reproducible, sourced, no hand-waving"

6 · Partner integration (2m) — don't name vendor

  • /enhance-run — a commercial enhancement / transcode partner in OWL's harness
  • Their product runs; OWL measures its energy honestly, same protocol
  • "vendors can prove efficiency claims on neutral ground"

7 · Field ↔ Bench (1m) — REM flash

  • REM context: GoS's sibling — real meters on real sites reporting continuously, where OWL's bench findings get checked against the wild
  • OWL = controlled bench; REM = fleet in the field
  • "bench tells you why, field tells you what's actually happening"

8 · Close (1m)

  • OWL turns "streaming is wasteful" from assertion → measurement
  • Open, reproducible, vendor-neutral
  • Ask: members, partners, contributors

Safety rails

  • Don't name the partner (use "enhancement partner")
  • Don't over-claim n — several findings are n=1, flagged as such; let the rigor be the story
  • "How accurate?" → point to the confidence flag, not a hand-wave
  • Keep carbon minimal — energy (W/Wh) is what GoS stands behind; CO₂e is reference-only
